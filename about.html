About & Acknowledgements

The Gita Archive Audio Libraries are an initiative to make the profound Sanskrit texts of Advaita Vedanta and related traditional knowledge systems highly accessible through modern Text-to-Speech (TTS) technologies.

Our Deepest Thanks To:

  • Text-to-Speech Engines: This project relies heavily on state-of-the-art open source TTS models that successfully synthesize Sanskrit phonetics. We extend our immense gratitude to Hear2Read and Vagdhenu for their outstanding contributions to Indian language speech synthesis.
  • Text Sources: The textual base for these audiobooks comes from the tireless digitization efforts of online Sanskrit communities. We thank sanskritdocuments.org and Sanskrit Wikisource for being the foundational pillars of open Sanskrit texts.
  • Institutional Support: We are deeply grateful for the guidance and support provided by Advaita Academy.
